Support – GraminisFelix

If you need help with GraminisFelix, please contact us by email.

Contact

Email: dejan@proledesign.com

What to Include

When contacting support, please include:

  • a short description of the issue
  • the email address used for your GraminisFelix account
  • your iPhone model and iOS version, if relevant
  • screenshots, if they help explain the issue

Premium Purchase Support

GraminisFelix Premium is handled through Apple’s in-app purchase system as a one-time unlock. For billing or refund questions, please use Apple Support.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app by going to Menu > Settings > Account > Delete Account.

ESP32 Watering Mode

ESP32 watering mode is optional. The app can also be used in standalone mode without external hardware.
