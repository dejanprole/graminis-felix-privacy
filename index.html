Privacy Policy – GraminisFelix

Effective date: January 6, 2026

GraminisFelix (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it.

Information We Collect

The App may collect account information such as your email address, username, password, garden name, and user ID in order to create and manage your account.

The App may also store garden watering data such as watering zone names, watering durations, watering history, and optional ESP32 watering settings if you choose to use ESP32 watering mode.

If you purchase GraminisFelix Premium, subscription and transaction information may be processed to verify your Premium access.

How We Use Your Information

  • create and manage your user account
  • enable core app functionality
  • save and display your watering zones and watering history
  • verify Premium subscription access
  • support optional ESP32 watering functionality, if enabled by the user
  • communicate essential app-related information, if applicable

The App does not use your personal information for third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics purposes.

Data Sharing

Your personal information is not sold, rented, or distributed to third parties.

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. GraminisFelix may use Apple-provided transaction information only to verify Premium access.

Data Storage

Your account information and watering data are stored only for as long as necessary to provide the App’s functionality.

Authentication tokens may be stored securely on your device to keep you signed in.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple services such as StoreKit for in-app purchases and subscriptions.

The App does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

User Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your account information and app data at any time by contacting us.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
